\section{Discussion} \label{sec:discussion}

A central implication of this work is that achieving rapid capability integration under a MOSA requires enhancing interface specifications with executable semantic-behavioral contracts.  Traditional Interface Control Documents (ICDs) and data-model alignments verify what we formalize as Tier~1 compatibility: ensuring that Fields are structurally present and matched in Color $\kappa$ (including UDDL $(C, L, P)$, runtime representation $\rho$, and provenance).  As our empirical evaluation demonstrates across Scenarios S2--S7 and S15 (Tables~\ref{tab:offline-rl} and~\ref{tab:offline-holdout}), however, Tier~1 Color alignment and Tier~2 kinematic compatibility are necessary but insufficient for system integration.  A mediation pipeline that matches every $\kappa$ attribute and bridges push-to-pull transfer modes can nonetheless fail at the trajectory level (e.g., by retaining stale samples across quiet intervals, introducing phase lag that violates downstream control deadlines, or misordering non-commutative physical operands).

By unifying a semantic-behavioral Color space with Wymore's T3SD coupling algebra and uLSCs, \emph{The Integration Game} makes these cross-cutting integration failure modes decidable.  Rather than relying on manual authoring discipline or post-hoc integration testing to catch behavioral discrepancies, the Winning Condition embeds Color typing, kinematic flow compatibility, and trajectory-level contract satisfaction into a formal verification procedure.

\subsection{Cost Structure and Learning-Guided Architecture Search} \label{sec:disc-cost}

In classical systems engineering trade studies, evaluating candidate architectures requires balancing feasibility constraints against multi-objective performance and cost figures of merit~\cite{Wymore1993}.  When automated search or reinforcement learning agents optimize a composite scalar objective, poorly structured cost functionals risk reward hacking: an agent may leave Consumer Needs unconnected to avoid operator placement costs, instantiate disconnected interior blocks that artificially reduce naive semantic distance metrics, or accept minor behavioral contract breaches in exchange for lower static complexity.

The axioms developed in Section~\ref{sec:meth-cost} are designed to prevent these pathologies.  \emph{Only Count Connected Moves} (A2) ensures that interior Mediation Operators cannot reduce potential unless they are causally connected to upstream Producers.  \emph{Connection Continuity} (A4) and \emph{Prefer Useful Moves} (A3) provide a smooth, dense shaping landscape for multi-hop graph assembly, while \emph{Feasibility Dominance} (A1) via $\Omega > \overline{C}_{\text{static}} + \overline{C}_{\text{dyn}}$ guarantees that no static or dynamic cost savings can compensate for a Tier~3 Hot Breach.  As the ablation results in Table~\ref{tab:ablations} confirm, both the Markovian progress signals supplied by the uLSC Reward Machine and the lexicographic separation enforced by $\Omega$ are essential for sample-efficient convergence to valid mediation architectures.  Moreover, parameterizing the policy over ontology-grounded relational compatibility features $\phi(s, a)$ enables zero-shot structural transfer to unseen component catalogs (Table~\ref{tab:offline-holdout}), demonstrating that the agent learns generalizable rules of semantic and kinematic mediation rather than memorizing scenario-specific connection sequences.

\subsection{Decidability Boundaries} \label{sec:disc-decidability}

Proposition~\ref{prop:finite-decidable} establishes first that when $TYR_{\mathcal{K}}$ comprises finite-state components over finite platform alphabets $A_{\kappa}$, the Coupled Resultant $Z_b$ is a finite-state Moore automaton; its product with the uLSC monitor $\mathcal{M}_{\text{LSC}}$ has a finite state space, so Breach reachability (and therefore $(Z_b, s_0) \models_T \mathcal{L}(IOR)$ / $(Z_b, s_0) \models_T IOR$) is decidable even for infinite horizons $OLR = \infty$.  Finite technologies are therefore not an undecidability barrier for universal behavioral certification.

Second, decidability in principle does not yield tractability in practice.  Exhaustive enumeration of alphabets is generally infeasible, and restricting evaluation to a finite suite $ITR_{\text{test}}$ exposes coincidence-point overfitting (Table~\ref{tab:scale}'s sub-millisecond rollouts remain essential for RL, but are incomplete certificates).  Idealized transforms over $\mathbb{R}$ can leave the general nonlinear unbounded case undecidable.  Restricting interior Mediation Operators to bounded memory and Tarski-decidable fragments ($\mathsf{QF\_LRA}$, $\mathsf{QF\_NRA}$, $\mathsf{QF\_LIRA}$) restores effective symbolic certification.  Pairing fast concrete rollouts with a terminal CEGIS gate~\cite{SolarLezama2008Sketch, AlurEtAl2013SyGuS} therefore bridges sample-efficient learning and exact fragment verification (Table~\ref{tab:ablations}), with SMT backends such as Z3 relegated to the Results implementation.

\subsection{Threats to Validity} \label{sec:disc-limitations}

\paragraph{Construct validity.}
Two modeling simplifications bound what we claim to measure.  Conceptual context $C$ is represented as semantic paths (dot notation) rather than the full SQL-like UDDL query grammar; extending to conjunctive UDDL queries (e.g., via near-semiring representations~\cite{mckibben2026nearring}) is a natural next step.  Likewise, operator complexity $c_{\text{op}}(v)$ and flow acceleration $a = \Delta v / \Delta t$ are modeled via abstracted catalog weights and discrete rate differentials rather than hardware-in-the-loop WCET profiling or continuous fluid-queue calculus.

\paragraph{Internal validity.}
Following Wymore's discrete-system formalism, $Z_b = \mathrm{RSY}(\mathcal{P})$ evolves on a synchronous global tick, and symbolic CEGIS abstracts floating-point platform arithmetic over idealized mathematical reals $\mathbb{R}$ ($\mathsf{QF\_LRA}$, $\mathsf{QF\_NRA}$).  Asynchronous clock drift across distributed buses (e.g., DEVS~\cite{ZeiglerPraehoferKim2000, wach2021devs} or timed automata) and IEEE-754 rounding corner cases ($\mathsf{QF\_FP}$) are not captured in the current symbolic gate.

\paragraph{External validity.}
Scenarios~S1--S15 isolate fifteen integration failure modes and include a FACE/SOSA-inspired avionics holdout, but they are synthetic benchmarks over compact catalogs ($6$--$11$ operators per winning Play).  Scaling to industrial-size systems with hundreds of UDDL entities and legacy ICDs will likely require pairing relational RL policies with Graph Neural Network encoders~\cite{Battaglia2018GN}, multiplex ontology alignment~\cite{mckibben2026multiplex, mckibben2026alignment}, and hierarchical uLSC composition.
