\section{Conclusions}

The proposed representation retains the T3SD definitions. The IOR determines
functionality, the technology and coupling recipe determine buildability, and
Wymore's homomorphism supplies the implementation relation. The characteristic
boundary fragment gives an equivalent assertional presentation of the
functionality cotyledon.

For implementation work, a chosen functional design supplies $Z_{\mathrm{ref}}$
and its dynamics fragment. Component checks, compositional lifting, and
invariance under re-encoding allow established results to be carried into larger
and more detailed designs. The run-length case shows the complete path and keeps
pipeline latency and clock granularity explicit at the boundary.

The practical consequence is that Wymore's cotyledons remain the design
spaces, while temporal fragments are the artifacts that are inspected, reused,
and, when finite, discharged by existing checkers. The method scales with
architecture to the extent that obligations can be localized and lifted. What
remains is to treat non-lock-step conformance, liveness, and measured industrial
use as separate developments, each with its own relation or evidence, rather
than as omitted clauses of $\Phi_{\mathrm{dyn}}$.
