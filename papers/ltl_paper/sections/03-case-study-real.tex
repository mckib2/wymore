\subsection{Real-Valued Accumulator}
\label{sec:case-real}

\paragraph{Reference system $Z_{\mathbb{R}}$.}
Behavior: discrete-time running sum. Start at $0$; on real input $u$ replace the state by $s+u$; output the current sum. (The clock is discrete; values are real.)

\paragraph{Compiled $\Phi_{\mathrm{dyn}}$.}
For every $s,u\in\mathbb{R}$:
\begin{align*}
  &\text{(readout)} && G\bigl(\mathit{state}(s) \rightarrow \mathit{output}(s)\bigr), \\
  &\text{(guided)} && G\bigl((\mathit{state}(s)\wedge\mathit{input}(u)) \rightarrow X(\mathit{state}(s{+}u))\bigr), \\
  &\text{(autonomous)} && G\bigl((\mathit{state}(s)\wedge\mathit{input}(\mathrm{none})) \rightarrow X(\mathit{state}(s))\bigr).
\end{align*}

\paragraph{Lifecycle iterations (condensed).}
\textit{v1} is identical to Table~\ref{tab:z-real}. \textit{v2} elaborates state to $(s,\mathrm{last})$ with $\delta((s,\ell),u)=(s{+}u,u)$ and $\rho=s$; optionally the output may be enriched to $(s,\mathrm{alarm})$ with $h_O=\pi_1$. \textit{v3} adds ticks and an energy channel $e\mapsto e+u^{2}$, with $h_S(s,\ell,t,e)=s$. In each case maps remain projections onto the sum: $\Phi_{Z_{\mathbb{R}}}$ is re-checked unchanged, while the classical witness stays structure-specific. Exact real arithmetic is used in the formalization; floating-point is not identified with $\mathbb{R}$, and unbounded real FO-LTL checking is not claimed to be decidable.

\paragraph{Workflow contrast.}
\textit{Assertional:} same $\Phi_{Z_{\mathbb{R}}}$ across v1--v3. \textit{Classical:} each elaboration needs a fresh confirmation that the chosen projection still preserves $\delta$ and $\rho$. Unlike the recognizer's shift register, $h_S$ here remains a projection---this subsection only shows that the maintenance pattern extends to real-valued state under a discrete clock.
