\section{Conclusions}

In this paper, we offer an assertional reinterpretation of Wymore's Functionality Cotyledon: functional verification as temporal property checking over trajectories, with trace semantics aligned to T3SD. We give a canonical FO-LTL encoding of DSYSTEM execution and discuss when solver-based checking offers a practical alternative to interactive homomorphism proofs. When implementations change structure, assertional re-checking of $\Phi$ can avoid rebuilding $Z_{\text{spec}}$ and $h$. Logical equivalence between paradigms, autonomous-step semantics, MBSE tool integration, and an illustrative case study remain open.
