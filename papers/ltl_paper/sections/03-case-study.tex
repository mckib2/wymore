\section{Case Study}
\label{sec:case-study}

We want to demonstrate how characteristic IOR fragments can be translated into temporal clauses. We develop a specification for a streaming run-length encoder implemented by a tape, head, state register, and instruction table. The complete resultant is related to $Z_{\mathrm{ref}}$ by an explicit homomorphism and by the compiled dynamics fragment.
