\section{Case Study: Streaming Run-Length Encoder}
\label{sec:case-study}

To demonstrate how temporal logic bridges the functionality cotyledon $\mathrm{CTL}(IOR)$ and the buildability cotyledon $\mathrm{CTL}(TYR)$ in practice, we develop a complete verification case study around a streaming Run-Length Encoder (RLE) assembled from four coupled discrete components: an input tape, a read/act head, an instruction table, and an output register. We trace the design from an idealized infinite-tape requirement $IOR_\infty$ to a family of finite-tape buildable machines $Z_{b,N}=\mathrm{RSY}(SCR_N)\in\mathrm{CTL}(TYR_N)$, isolate their operational system modes $Z_{m,N}\in\mathrm{SYSMO}(Z_{b,N})$, and verify membership in the implementability cotyledon $\mathrm{CTL}(IOR_N,TYR_N)$ automatically using propositional LTL model checking.
