\section{Case Study}
\label{sec:case-study}

Three boundary laws show how characteristic IOR fragments become temporal
clauses. The run-length law is then implemented by a tape, head, state register,
and instruction table. Its boundary IOR records the latency of the coupled
pipeline. The complete resultant is related to $Z_{\mathrm{ref}}$ by an explicit
homomorphism and by the compiled dynamics fragment. Re-encoding and clock
counterexamples test the scope of that fragment.
