\section{Case Study: Run-Length Encoder}
\label{sec:case-study}

We develop a verification case study around a streaming Run-Length Encoder (RLE) assembled from four coupled discrete components: an input tape ($Z_0$), a sense/act head ($Z_1$), an output register ($Z_2$), and an instruction table ($Z_3$). We begin with an idealized infinite-tape requirement $IOR_\infty$ in $\mathrm{CTL}(IOR_\infty)$, refine the design to a family of finite-tape buildable machines $Z_{b,N}=\mathrm{RSY}(SCR_N)\in\mathrm{CTL}(TYR_N)$, isolate their operational system modes $Z_{m,N}\in\mathrm{SYSMO}(Z_{b,N})$, and verify membership in the implementability cotyledon $\mathrm{CTL}(IOR_N,TYR_N)$ using propositional LTL model checking.
