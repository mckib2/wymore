\subsection{Cascade Composition: Count \texorpdfstring{$0111001110$}{0111001110} Accepts with Readout \texorpdfstring{$3n$}{3n}}
\label{sec:case-compose}

The preceding ladders treat each reference in isolation. T3SD buildability is often about the opposite situation: a restricted kit of available subsystems must be wired so that their interaction realizes a coupled functional intent. The kit already developed is: ones-counters, the string recognizer for $01110$ (including its shift-register v3), the dual-port select among $01$ and $01110$, and the real accumulator. That kit has no shelf zone for the ten-bit concatenation $0111001110$, and no primitive counter whose readout is $3n$. The cascade below assembles both: a double-$01110$ recognizer from two shelf matchers plus a ones-counter arm, cascaded into a $3n$ counter assembled from three ones-counters and the real accumulator as summing block. Implementability is established by an explicit Wymore homomorphism (equivalently, by satisfaction of the compiled dynamics-encoding fragment). Figures~\ref{fig:cascade-spec}--\ref{fig:cascade-impl} show the reference wiring and the coupled buildable.

\paragraph{Zones and cascade recipe.}
Let $Z_{\mathrm{rec}}$ recognize $0111001110$ by matching $01110$ then $01110$, resetting after each full accept so later occurrences can be counted. Let $Z_{\mathrm{cnt}}$ count full-string accepts by $n\mapsto n{+}1$ on each accept, with readout $3n$. The cascade feeds the external Bit stream into $Z_{\mathrm{rec}}$ and wires each full-string accept into $Z_{\mathrm{cnt}}$ (Figure~\ref{fig:cascade-spec}). Write $Z_{12}$ for the resulting cascade. Plain-language intent: \emph{if $n$ is the number of $0111001110$ accepts so far, output $3n$}.

Compiled $\Phi_{Z_{12}}$ is the dynamics-encoding fragment of this monolithic cascade table: guided steps of the sequential double-$01110$ recognizer together with the accept-count update and readout $3n$. Checking uses the usual hom-relative atoms on the projected recognizer state and count.

\paragraph{What is available vs.\ what must be built.}
Neither subsystem is a single shelf zone. Assemble $Z_{\mathrm{rec}}$ from two shelf $01110$ matchers and a ones-counter arm (Bit fan-out; second matcher held until armed; clear on full accept). Assemble $Z_{\mathrm{cnt}}$ from three ones-counters that share the accept input (fan-out) and the real accumulator as the summing block: each tick the accumulator re-sums the three ones outputs from $0$ by three add steps, $((0{+}n_1){+}n_2){+}n_3$. Under lockstep, that equals $3n$.

\paragraph{Cascade coupling.}
The awkward buildable $Z_{12}^{\mathrm{impl}}$ couples those two assemblies (Figure~\ref{fig:cascade-impl}): Bit into the double-$01110$ wiring; each full-string accept fans into the three-ones$+$accumulator $3n$ block; cascade readout is the accumulator state.

\paragraph{Workflow contrast.}
\textit{Assertional:} $\Phi_{Z_{12}}$ is compiled once from the cascade table. Verifying $Z_{12}^{\mathrm{impl}}$ means re-checking that fragment (equivalently, exhibiting/verifying a Def.~4.3 witness). Changing either assembly re-runs the cascade check against the same $\Phi_{Z_{12}}$.
\textit{Classical:} a human must construct $h_S$ mapping the coupled recognizer$\times$triple-ones state onto phase$\times$progress$\times$accept-count and re-prove that the summed readout equals $3n$. Replacing either assembly forces that cascade witness to be rebuilt.

The point is qualitative T3SD buildability under an honest technology bound: assemble the missing ten-bit recognizer and the missing $3n$ counter from kit parts, and couple them---so assertional satisfaction of $\Phi_{Z_{12}}$ holds---while classical maintenance tracks the new wiring inside the coupled witness.
