\subsection{Basic Kit: Counter, Subtracter, Adder, and Zero-Test}
\label{sec:case-basics}

\paragraph{Counter $Z_{\mathrm{inc}}$.}
Behavior in plain language: start at count $0$; on enable $1$ increment; on enable $0$ hold; the output at every tick is the current count.

\paragraph{Compiled $\Phi_{\mathrm{dyn}}$.}
For every $n\in\mathbb{N}$:
\begin{align*}
  &\text{(readout)} && G\bigl(\mathit{state}(n) \rightarrow \mathit{output}(n)\bigr), \\
  &\text{(guided $1$)} && G\bigl((\mathit{state}(n)\wedge\mathit{input}(1)) \rightarrow X(\mathit{state}(n{+}1))\bigr), \\
  &\text{(guided $0$)} && G\bigl((\mathit{state}(n)\wedge\mathit{input}(0)) \rightarrow X(\mathit{state}(n))\bigr), \\
  &\text{(autonomous)} && G\bigl((\mathit{state}(n)\wedge\mathit{input}(\mathrm{none})) \rightarrow X(\mathit{state}(n))\bigr).
\end{align*}
Denote this family by $\Phi_{Z_{\mathrm{inc}}}$. Hom-relative checking uses $\mathit{specState}(n)$ meaning ``the buildable's projected count equals $n$.''

\paragraph{Direct buildable.}
$Z_{\mathrm{inc}}^{\mathrm{dir}}$ is identical to Table~\ref{tab:z-inc}. The homomorphism is the identity: $h_S=h_I=h_O=\mathrm{id}$. By the bi-implication, $Z_{\mathrm{inc}}^{\mathrm{dir}}\models_{\mathrm{dyn}}\Phi_{Z_{\mathrm{inc}}}$.

\paragraph{Adder $Z_{\mathrm{add}}$.}
Behavior: Nat accumulator with commands $\mathit{hold}$, $\mathit{clear}$, and $\mathit{add}(u)$. Clear resets to $0$; add accumulates; readout is the running sum.

\paragraph{Zero-test $Z_{\mathrm{jz}}$.}
Behavior: Moore latch whose next state (and readout) is whether the sampled Nat equals $0$. Plain-language intent: \emph{branch on zero}.

Together these four zones are the shelf kit used below: increment, decrement, accumulate-with-clear, and zero-test. Each has a compiled $\Phi_{\mathrm{dyn}}$ and a verified identity buildable.
